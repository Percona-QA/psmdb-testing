# check_async_oplog_sampling_truncates_new_writes: truncation wait expires

PSMDB 9.0.4-1; suite: no_passthrough.

**Failure:** final assert.soon reaches its 90-second deadline while a document inserted during paused sampling remains in the oplog. One execution failed in 102.59 seconds.

**Evidence:** sampling completed and later writes produced 50 truncate markers, but truncateCount remained zero. Oplog size was approximately 23 MB against a 1 MB limit.

**Unresolved cause:** inspect the oldest marker, pinned oplog timestamp and cap-maintainer wait state. The periodic recheck interval is 300 seconds; a pin advancing after writes finish could exceed the assertion deadline. This explanation was not proven. The outer resmoke timeout does not change assert.soon.

**Required investigation:** determine the actual pin/wakeup condition before changing timing or excluding the test. The restart also replaces minOplogTruncationPoints=2 with default 10; its causal role is unverified.

**Status:** remains enabled; no control run or source change.
